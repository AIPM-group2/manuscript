# Pediatric Radiology (Springer) - Submission Guidelines

## 1. General Formatting
- **File Format**: MS Word (.docx) or LaTeX.
- **Font**: Normal, plain font (e.g., 10-point Times Roman).
- **Line Spacing**: Double-spaced.
- **Margins**: Standard margins (not specified, imply 1 inch/2.54 cm).
- **Page Numbering**: Automatic page numbering required.
- **Headings**: No more than three levels of displayed headings.
- **Abbreviations**: Defined at first mention and used consistently thereafter.

## 2. Manuscript Types & Limits

### Original Article
- **Page Limit**: Max 18 double-spaced pages (approx. 4,500 words) excluding tables/figures.
- **Abstract**: Structured (150-250 words).
- **Structure**:
  1. Introduction
  2. Materials and methods
  3. Results
  4. Discussion
  5. Conclusion

### Review
- **Page Limit**: Max 20 double-spaced pages (approx. 5,000 words).
- **Abstract**: Paragraph form (unstructured).
- **Structure**: Introduction, Headings (topic-based).

### Case Report
- **Page Limit**: Max 6 double-spaced pages.
- **References**: Limited to 8.
- **Abstract**: Paragraph form (<125 words).
- **Structure**: Introduction, Case Report, Discussion.

### Technical Innovation
- **Page Limit**: Max 6 double-spaced pages.
- **References**: Limited to 8.
- **Abstract**: Paragraph form (<125 words).

### Pictorial Essay
- **Page Limit**: Max 9 double-spaced pages (approx. 2,250 words).
- **Figures**: Up to 30 figure parts.
- **Abstract**: Paragraph form.

## 3. Title Page (Separate File)
*Must be separate for double-blind review.*
- **Title**: Concise and informative.
- **Authors**: Names and affiliations.
- **Corresponding Author**: E-mail address.
- **ORCID**: 16-digit ORCID if available.
- **Declarations**: Funding, competing interests, ethics approval.

## 4. Abstract Requirements
- **Original Article**: Structured into:
  - Background
  - Objective
  - Materials and methods
  - Results
  - Conclusion
- **Word Count**: 150-250 words (for Original Articles).
- **Content**: No undefined abbreviations, no unspecified references.

## 5. Keywords
- **Count**: 4 to 6 keywords.
- **Purpose**: For indexing (MeSH terms recommended).

## 6. Scientific Style
- **Units**: International System of Units (SI).
- **Math**: Use Equation Editor or MathType.

## 7. Citations & References
- **In-Text Style**: Numbered in square brackets, e.g., `[1]`, `[1–3, 5]`.
- **Reference List**: Numbered consecutively in order of appearance.
- **Format Examples**:
  - *Journal*: Smith J, Jones M Jr (1999) Future of health. N Engl J Med 965:325–329.
  - *DOI*: Always include DOI links if available (https://doi.org/...).
  - *Journal Names*: Abbreviated according to ISSN LTWA (e.g., *Pediatr Radiol*).

## 8. Figures & Tables
- **Figure Numbering**: Arabic numerals (Fig. 1, Fig. 2).
- **Figure Captions**: Begin with bold **Fig. 1**, no punctuation after number. Included in text file, not in figure file.
- **Table Numbering**: Arabic numerals (Table 1, Table 2).
- **Table Captions**: Title explaining components, placed above table.
- **Citations**: Figures/Tables must be cited in text in consecutive order.
- **Resolution**:
  - Line art: 1200 dpi
  - Halftones: 300 dpi
  - Combination: 600 dpi

## 9. Ethical Declarations
- **Required Sections**:
  - Competing Interests
  - Funding
  - Ethical Approval
  - Informed Consent
  - Data Availability Statement

## 10. Double-Blind Review Policy
- Manuscript must be anonymized.
- No author names or affiliations in the main text.
- No self-citation phrases like "we previously showed [1]" (use "Smith et al. [1] showed").
